# Privacy notice: draft to adapt

This is a draft notice for a precast concrete manufacturer that uses this ERP. To use it:

1. Replace everything in `[square brackets]`.
2. Have a lawyer read it.
3. Enter it in **Data Protection > Notice & contact**. The purposes map one-to-one onto the notice's purpose list. The sections below them go in the "Anything else" field.

Keep it short and plain. Rule 3 asks for a notice a person can understand without reading anything else. Translate it into the languages your workers and customers read.

---

## How [Company name] uses your personal data

**[Company name]**, [registered address], makes and sells precast concrete products. This notice explains what personal data we hold about you, why we hold it, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do. It applies to our staff, the labour and contractors who work at our plants, our customers and suppliers who are individuals, and people who make an enquiry.

We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.

### What we use your data for

**If you work for us (staff)**
- **Employment administration.** We use your name, employee code, work email, phone, address, gender, dates of joining and leaving, attendance, leave requests and the HR documents you give us. We use them to employ you, pay you and meet labour law. *Basis:* employment purposes (Act s.7(i)). *Kept:* for your employment, then as long as labour, tax and provident fund law require.
- **Sign-in and security records.** We record your sign-in times, IP address, browser, and the changes you make in our systems. This protects the business and its records. *Basis:* employment purposes (s.7(i)). *Kept:* at least one year (Rules r.6).

**If you work at our plants (labour and contractors)**
- **Engaging and paying you.** We use your name, phone, address, Aadhaar, date of birth, gender, bank account, ESIC and UAN numbers, emergency contact, attendance, wages and advances. We use them to identify you, pay you, and meet wage, ESI and EPF law. *Basis:* employment purposes and data you gave us for this purpose (s.7(i), s.7(a)). *Kept:* while you work with us, then as long as wage, ESI and EPF law require. After that your identity, bank and contact details are erased; your name stays in the wage register.

**If you buy from us or supply us (customers and suppliers)**
- **Orders, delivery and billing.** We use your name, phone, email, billing and delivery address, GSTIN and PAN. *Basis:* data you gave us for this purpose (s.7(a)); GST law. *Kept:* invoices and accounts for as long as GST and income-tax law require (at least 72 months after the annual return, and 8 years for accounts).
- **Paying suppliers.** We use your name, bank account, IFSC, beneficiary name, PAN and GSTIN. *Basis:* s.7(a); income-tax law (TDS). *Kept:* as long as tax law requires.

**If you made an enquiry**
- **Following up your enquiry.** We use your name, contact person, phone, email, city and requirement to call or write to you and send a quotation. *Basis:* your consent. *Kept:* until the enquiry closes, then [1 year]. You can withdraw consent at any time.

**Optional**
- **Offers and product updates.** We use your name, phone and email to tell you about new products and prices. *Basis:* your consent, which you may refuse without it affecting anything you buy from us.

### Your rights

You can ask us to:

- **tell you** what personal data we hold about you and what we use it for;
- **correct or update** it if it is wrong or incomplete;
- **erase** it when we no longer need it. We will keep only what the law requires, such as your name on invoices or the wage register;
- **stop using it** for anything you consented to, by withdrawing consent. This is as easy as giving it;
- **name someone** to exercise these rights for you if you die or cannot act yourself;
- **hear a grievance** about how we handled your data.

**How to ask:**
- **Staff:** My Profile > My privacy in the ERP.
- **Everyone else:** [https://your-erp-address/privacy/your-code], email, letter, or in person at any of our plants.

We will check who you are before we act, and reply within **[30] days**.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the **Data Protection Board of India**.

### Children

We do not knowingly engage anyone under 18 at our plants. Where we hold a child's data, we hold it only with the verified consent of a parent or guardian.
